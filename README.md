# Prostovoljec

**Prostovoljec** is a web-based platform dedicated to connecting passionate volunteers with organizations and agencies supporting people in need. Our mission is to make volunteering accessible, easy, and impactful—helping individuals discover meaningful opportunities and empowering organizations to reach more helping hands.

---

## 🌟 Project Description

Prostovoljec serves as a bridge between volunteers and organizations. Whether you want to lend a hand at local shelters, contribute to community events, or offer specialized skills, Prostovoljec helps you find opportunities that match your interests and availability.

We believe that volunteering should be simple and rewarding. By streamlining the process of finding and joining opportunities, we aim to foster a culture of giving and support within our communities.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Browse Opportunities:** Volunteers can easily explore available opportunities by category, location, or organization.
- **Simple Sign-Up:** Quick and user-friendly registration and profile creation for volunteers.
- **Opportunity Details:** Clear descriptions, requirements, and contact information for each listing.
- **Organization Dashboard:** Agencies can post, manage, and update their volunteering opportunities.
- **Responsive Design:** Optimized for desktop and mobile use.
- **Notifications:** Volunteers receive updates about new opportunities and application statuses.

## 📖 Usage Guide

### For Volunteers

1. **Browse** available opportunities by navigating the home page or using the search/filter tools.
2. **Sign up** or log in to your account.
3. **View details** of opportunities that interest you.
4. **Apply** directly through the platform or follow the organization’s contact instructions.
5. **Track your applications** and receive notifications about your status.

### For Organizations & Agencies

1. **Register** as an organization.
2. **Post new opportunities** by providing clear descriptions, requirements, and contact details.
3. **Manage applications** from volunteers and update opportunity statuses.
4. **Connect with volunteers** who are eager to help!
